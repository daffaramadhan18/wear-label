The small uppercase camel label that sits above a heading.

11px in camel (`--color-ink-subtle`) at 0.18em tracking. Use it to name a section
above its heading — never as a heading itself, since it carries no heading
semantics.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>New arrivals</Eyebrow>
```
